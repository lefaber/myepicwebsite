I Will Not Use AI for Creative Projects

This will be a short post, just announcing that I intend to never use AI in any of my creative projects. This might be a bold statement, or it might not, I just want to be clear about this from the beginning.

I don't deny that AI is really efficient at performing certain work, and when you just want quick answers it can be very useful. That being said, I believe that a medium intended to express human emotion and creativity should be made by humans, otherwise it loses its greatest value - the humanity.

There's a risk using absolute words like "never", and I don't know what the future will bring. I just don't want AI to aid in any major way or shape the process of creativity, for my projects at least.

So yeah, that's that. Wish you a great day!
